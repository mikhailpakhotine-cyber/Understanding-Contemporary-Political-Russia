## Academic Integrity

### Core Principle
All work must be your own. Collaboration is encouraged for studying and discussion, but all graded work must be completed independently.

### Violations Include
- Cheating
- Plagiarism
- Falsification
- Unauthorized collaboration

### Consequences
- F for assignment (minimum)
- Possible F for course
- "Punitive F" cannot be removed from transcript
- Multiple violations = F for course

### Resources
- [Anti-Plagiarism Guide](../resources/anti-plagiarism-guide.md)
- Turnitin on Canvas
- Writing Center
- Office hours

## Plagiarism

### Definition
Using others' words, ideas, or work without proper attribution.

### Zero Tolerance
- Drafts: Opportunity to learn and fix
- Final submissions: Penalties apply immediately
- No "I didn't know" excuses

### Prevention
- Cite all sources
- Use quotation marks for exact wording
- Paraphrase correctly
- Start early
- Use Writing Center

## Late Work

### Standard Penalty
- **2 points deducted per calendar day late**
- Must be submitted before next exam/assignment or last class session

### Exceptions
- Serious, documented excuses
- University-approved activities (documented in advance)
- Use of late pass (one time only)

### Deadlines
- Late assignments not accepted after next exam/assignment due date
- Late assignments not accepted after last regular class session

## Exam Make-Ups

### Excused (No Penalty)
- Arranged in advance, OR
- Serious and documented excuse

### Unexcused (With Penalty)
- Point deduction of at least one letter grade
- Student must contact professor
- Must complete before next exam/assignment

### Final Exams
- Can only be made up with serious, documented excuse
- Must arrange in advance if possible

## Late Pass

### What It Is
- ONE free late pass for the semester
- Works for assignments OR exams
- One-week extension penalty-free

### How to Use
- Email professor by due date/exam date
- State you're invoking late pass
- Extension granted automatically

### Important
- Only one per semester
- Don't waste it unnecessarily
- Save for emergencies if possible

## Attendance

### Expectations
- Regular, prompt attendance
- Arrive on time
- Stay for full class
- Be present and engaged

### Impact on Grades
- Directly affects participation score (15 points)
- No distinction between excused/unexcused for participation grading
- Exception: Extended medical absences

### Student-Athletes
- Excused for competitions only
- NOT excused for practices, meetings, off-season
- Must make up work in advance

### Recording Attendance
- Student responsibility to be marked present if late
- Exam days don't affect participation score

## Participation

### Requirements
- Active engagement in discussions
- Participation in group activities
- Positive contributions
- Respectful behavior

### Grading
- See [Participation Rubric](../assignments/participation/rubric.md)
- Attendance: 7 points
- Quality: 8 points
- Total: 15 points

### Expectations
- Come prepared
- Contribute thoughtfully
- Listen actively
- Respect others
- Support opinions with evidence

## Technology

### Permitted
- Laptops/tablets for educational use
- Note-taking
- Accessing readings

### Prohibited
- Non-class activities
- Social media
- Gaming
- Email checking
- Texting
- Distracting others

### Recording
- Requires written permission in advance
- No photos or video
- Audio recording only with permission

### Consequences
- Warning
- Loss of technology privileges
- Lowered participation grade
- Possible removal from course

## Disruptive Behavior

### Definition
Substantial disruption of class activities

### Examples
- Inappropriate talking
- Refusing instructions
- Disrespecting others
- Monopolizing discussions
- Offensive language
- Cell phone disruptions
- Inappropriate technology use

### Consequences
- Warning
- Lowered participation grade
- Failing participation grade
- Failing course grade
- Removal from course

### Serious Violations
Bullying, threats, verbal/physical abuse:
- Immediate removal from course
- Student Conduct proceedings

## Writing Requirements

### Format
- Typed
- Double-spaced
- 10- or 12-point font
- Standard (1-inch) margins

### Length
- Must meet minimum
- Cannot exceed maximum by more than 1 page
- Violations result in D or lower

### Content
- Focus on course material
- Reference course readings
- Outside sources supplement, not replace

## Citations

### Requirements
- All sources must be cited
- Use accepted style (APA or MLA)
- Consistent throughout
- Complete documentation

### Grading Impact
- No citations: F
- Incomplete: D
- Incorrect style: C
- Inconsistent: C
- Wikipedia: One letter grade penalty

### Resources
- [Citation Guides](../resources/citation-guides/)
- Writing Center
- Purdue OWL
- Library

## Submission

### Where
- Canvas only (not email)
- Keep backup copies

### When
- By deadline (usually 11:59 PM)
- Submit early to avoid technical issues

### Turnitin
- All papers checked for plagiarism
- Submit drafts early
- Review similarity report
- Revise as needed

## Accommodations

### Disability Resource Center (DRC)
- Register with DRC
- Initiate accommodation release (Clockwork)
- Notify professor
- Remind professor before exams/assignments

### Types of Accommodations
- Testing accommodations
- Note-taking assistance
- Extended time
- Other approved accommodations

## Military/Veterans

### Support
- Combat exposure may require accommodations
- Register with DRC
- Discuss needs with professor

## Important Reminders

### Email Policy
- Check campus email regularly
- Class announcements sent via email
- No grade information via email (confidentiality)
- No assignment submission via email

### Mandatory Reporting
- Faculty must report crimes to University Police
- Includes sexual misconduct
- See Clery Act and Title IX information

### Questions About Policies
- Review syllabus first
- Check this policy directory
- Attend office hours
- Email specific questions

## Policy Changes

All policies are subject to change due to:
- Extenuating circumstances
- Mutual agreement between instructor and students
- University requirements

Changes will be announced via email and Canvas.
