# Week 3: History, Communism, and Its Demise
**Date**: September 9, 2024

## Overview
Examination of Russian history, the rise and fall of communism, and the transition from the Soviet Union.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand key periods in Russian history
- Analyze the Communist system and its ideology
- Examine the causes and consequences of the Soviet collapse

## Topics Covered
- Pre-Soviet Russian history
- Communist Revolution and Soviet state
- Soviet political and economic systems
- Factors leading to collapse
- Post-Soviet transition

## Required Readings
- Shiraev Ch. 2: History
- Shiraev Ch. 3: Communism and Its Demise
