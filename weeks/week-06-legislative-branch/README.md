# Week 6: Legislative Branch
**Date**: September 30, 2024

## Overview
Study of Russia's legislative branch, parliamentary system, and lawmaking process.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the structure of the Russian parliament
- Analyze the role of the legislative branch in Russian politics
- Examine the relationship between legislative and executive branches

## Topics Covered
- Federal Assembly structure (Duma and Federation Council)
- Electoral system for parliament
- Legislative process and powers
- Political parties in parliament
- Legislative-executive relations

## Required Readings
- Shiraev Ch. 5: Legislative Branch
