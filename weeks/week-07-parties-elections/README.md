# Week 7: Parties, Elections, and Participation
**Date**: October 7, 2024

## Overview
Examination of Russia's party system, electoral processes, and citizen political participation.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Russia's multi-party system
- Analyze electoral processes and their fairness
- Examine patterns of political participation

## Topics Covered
- Political party system in Russia
- Major political parties and their platforms
- Electoral laws and procedures
- Voter turnout and participation
- Civil society and political engagement
- Elections and democracy

## Required Readings
- Shiraev Ch. 7: Political Parties
- Shiraev Ch. 8: Elections
- Shiraev Ch. 9: Political Participation

## Preparation for Exam #2
Exam #2 is in two weeks (October 21) and will cover Weeks 5-7
