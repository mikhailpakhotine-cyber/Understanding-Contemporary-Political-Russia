# Week 4: Executive Branch and Federalism
**Date**: September 16, 2024

## Overview
Analysis of Russia's executive branch structure and federal system of government.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the structure and powers of Russia's executive branch
- Analyze the federal system and center-region relations
- Compare Russian executive power with other systems

## Topics Covered
- Presidential system in Russia
- Prime Minister and government structure
- Federal structure and regional governance
- Executive powers and limitations
- Constitutional framework

## Required Readings
- Shiraev Ch. 4: Executive Branch and Federalism
