# Week 10: Economic Transition and Policy
**Date**: November 4, 2024

## Overview
Analysis of Russia's transition from a command economy to market economy and contemporary economic policies.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Russia's economic transition from communism
- Analyze post-Soviet economic reforms and challenges
- Examine current economic policies and performance

## Topics Covered
- Soviet economic system
- Transition to market economy (1990s)
- Privatization and its consequences
- Economic crises and recovery
- Contemporary economic structure
- Energy economy and natural resources
- Economic policy making

## Required Readings
- Shiraev Ch. 10: Economic Transition and Policy
- Online Reading (see Canvas)
