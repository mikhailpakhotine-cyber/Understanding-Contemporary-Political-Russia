## Study Aids

### Glossary of Key Terms
- **Political Culture**: The attitudes, beliefs, and values that underpin a political system
- **Post-Soviet**: Referring to the period after the 1991 dissolution of the USSR
- **Federation**: A system of government where power is divided between central and regional authorities
- **Comparative Politics**: The study of political systems through comparison

### Study Questions
