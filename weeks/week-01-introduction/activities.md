# Week 1 Activities: Introduction/Overview

## In-Class Discussion Questions

### Opening Discussion
1. What images or ideas come to mind when you think of contemporary Russia?
2. What current events involving Russia are you aware of?
3. Why is it important to study Russian political culture?

### Textbook Discussion (Shiraev Ch. 1)
1. How does Shiraev define political culture?
2. What makes Russia's political system unique or distinctive?
3. What are the key challenges in studying contemporary Russia?

### Comparative Analysis
1. How does Russian political culture differ from American political culture?
2. What similarities might exist between the two systems?
3. How might Russia's history influence its contemporary politics?

### Course Framework
1. What are you most interested in learning about Russia this semester?
2. What questions do you hope the course will answer?
3. How can understanding Russia help us understand global politics?

## Small Group Activities

### Activity 1: Concept Mapping
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Group Size**: 3-4 students

Create a concept map of "Political Culture" based on the readings:
- Define the term
- Identify key components
- Show relationships between components
- Provide examples

**Share Out**: Each group presents their concept map (2 minutes per group)

### Activity 2: Current Events Analysis
**Time**: 20 minutes
**Group Size**: 3-4 students

1. Identify a recent news story about Russia
2. Discuss what the story reveals about Russian politics or society
3. Consider what questions the story raises
4. Identify what background knowledge you'd need to fully understand the story

**Share Out**: Brief reports from each group

### Activity 3: Comparison Exercise
**Time**: 15 minutes
**Pairs**

Create a Venn diagram comparing:
- Russian political culture (based on initial readings)
- American political culture (based on your knowledge)

Identify:
- Unique aspects of each
- Potential similarities
- Areas where you need more information
