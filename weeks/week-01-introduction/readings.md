## Additional Context

### Why These Readings Matter
Week 1 readings establish the foundation for the entire semester. They introduce:
- The analytical framework we'll use throughout the course
- Key terminology and concepts
- The comparative approach to studying Russia
- Contemporary relevance of Russian politics

### Preparation for Class Discussion
Be ready to discuss:
- Your initial impressions of Russian political culture
- How Russia compares to political systems you're familiar with
- Questions you have about the course material
- Current events related to Russia
