## Practice Questions

1. Describe Russia's court system and the role of the Constitutional Court.
2. What were the main challenges of Russia's economic transition in the 1990s?
3. How does Russia's social welfare system work?
4. What are Russia's main foreign policy objectives?
5. What challenges does Russia face in the coming decade?

4. Assess Russia's social policy challenges.
