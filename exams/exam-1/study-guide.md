## Study Strategies

### Review Materials
1. Shiraev Chapters 1-4
2. Week 1, 3, and 4 lecture notes
3. Online readings from these weeks
4. Class discussion notes
5. Any handouts or Canvas materials

### Effective Study Techniques
1. Create concept maps
2. Make flashcards for key terms
3. Outline each chapter's main arguments
4. Practice explaining concepts in your own words
5. Compare/contrast different systems
6. Connect historical events to current politics

### Study Questions
1. How does Shiraev define political culture?
2. What were the main causes of the Soviet Union's collapse?
3. How is Russia's executive branch structured?
4. What is the relationship between the President and Prime Minister?
5. How does Russian federalism work?
6. How does Russia's political culture differ from American political culture?
7. What were Gorbachev's reforms and why did they matter?
8. What challenges did Russia face in the 1990s transition?
