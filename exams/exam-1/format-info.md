## Exam Policies

### Academic Integrity
- All work must be your own
- No communication with other students during exam
- No looking at others' papers
- Violations will result in F for exam and possible F for course

### Late Arrival
- Arrive on time
- Late students may not be admitted after first student finishes
- Cannot make up time lost due to tardiness

### Early Finish
- May leave quietly when finished
- Turn in exam at front
- Do not disturb others

### During Exam
- Remain quiet
- No talking
- Raise hand if you have questions
- Stay in your seat except for emergencies
- No bathroom breaks unless emergency

## Make-Up Policy

### Excused Make-Ups (No Penalty)
Available ONLY if:
- Arranged in advance, OR
- Serious and documented excuse

### Unexcused Make-Ups (With Penalty)
- Point deduction equivalent to at least one letter grade
- Must contact professor to arrange
- Student's responsibility to schedule

### University-Approved Activities
- Must be documented and arranged in advance
- Exam must be taken in advance

### Late Pass
- Can use your ONE late pass for semester
- Must email professor by exam date
- Allows make-up without penalty

### Deadline for Make-Ups
- Must complete before next exam (Exam #2 on October 21)
- Must complete before next assignment due date
- Whichever is sooner

## Tips for Exam Day

### Night Before
- Review key concepts
- Get adequate sleep (7-8 hours)
- Don't cram all night
- Prepare materials to bring

### Morning of Exam
- Eat a good breakfast
- Arrive early
- Bring required materials
- Use restroom before class
- Stay calm and confident

### During Exam
1. **Read instructions carefully** - Don't assume
2. **Budget time** - Note point values
3. **Start with what you know** - Build confidence
4. **For essays**: Outline first, then write
5. **For multiple choice**: Eliminate wrong answers
6. **Check your work** - If time permits
7. **Don't panic** - Skip hard questions and return later

### After Exam
- Don't discuss answers with others
- Don't stress about what's done
- Focus on next material

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the exam cumulative?**
A: Exam #1 covers only Weeks 1-4. Later exams cover different material (not cumulative unless specified).

**Q: Can I bring notes?**
A: No, unless specifically announced as "open note."

**Q: What if I'm sick on exam day?**
A: Contact professor as soon as possible. Provide documentation. Arrange make-up.

**Q: Can I use my late pass for the exam?**
A: Yes, if you haven't already used it.

**Q: How specific do I need to be?**
A: Know key concepts, major events, important terms. Don't need to memorize every detail.

**Q: Will there be extra credit?**
A: No extra credit on exams.

**Q: What's the best way to study?**
A: Review all readings, create study guide, practice explaining concepts, study with others, attend office hours with questions.
