## Sample Answer: Key Term

**Example**: Define and explain "perestroika"

**Sample Answer**:
Perestroika was Mikhail Gorbachev's policy of economic restructuring in the Soviet Union during the mid-to-late 1980s. The term literally means "restructuring" in Russian. Gorbachev introduced perestroika to address the Soviet Union's economic stagnation by allowing some market mechanisms and decentralization of economic decision-making. Along with glasnost (openness), perestroika was intended to reform the Soviet system, but it ultimately contributed to the system's collapse by exposing deep structural problems and loosening central control.
