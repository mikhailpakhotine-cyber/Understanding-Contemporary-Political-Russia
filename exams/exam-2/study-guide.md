# Exam #2 Study Guide

**Date**: October 21, 2024
**Coverage**: Weeks 5-7
**Value**: 20 points

## Exam Coverage

### Week 5: Political Culture
- Russian political attitudes and beliefs
- Civic participation patterns
- Trust in government and institutions
- Political socialization
- Cultural values and politics
- **Reading**: Online Reading on Political Culture

### Week 6: Legislative Branch
- Federal Assembly structure (Duma and Federation Council)
- Electoral system for parliament
- Legislative process and powers
- Political parties in parliament
- Legislative-executive relations
- **Reading**: Shiraev Ch. 5

### Week 7: Parties, Elections, and Participation
- Political party system in Russia
- Major political parties and platforms
- Electoral laws and procedures
- Voter turnout and participation
- Civil society and political engagement
- **Readings**: Shiraev Ch. 7-9

## Key Concepts to Review

- Political culture components
- Trust in institutions
- Duma vs. Federation Council
- Electoral systems
- Party systems
- United Russia and other major parties
- Civil society
- Political participation types
- Voter turnout patterns
- Electoral integrity
