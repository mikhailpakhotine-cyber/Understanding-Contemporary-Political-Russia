## Sample Topics to Study

### Political Culture
- How do Russians view their government?
- What shapes Russian political attitudes?
- How does Russian political culture compare to American?

### Legislative Branch
- Structure of Federal Assembly
- Duma vs. Federation Council
- Legislative process

### Parties and Elections
- Major political parties
- Electoral systems
- Voter participation

## Practice Questions

1. Describe the structure of Russia's Federal Assembly.
2. What are the major political parties in Russia?
3. How does Russia's electoral system work?
4. What is political culture and how does it affect Russian politics?
5. Compare Russian and American patterns of political participation.

## Essay Topics

1. Analyze the role of political parties in contemporary Russia.
2. Discuss the legislative branch's relationship with the executive branch.
3. Evaluate the state of civil society in Russia.
