## Exam Policies

### Academic Integrity
- All work must be your own
- No communication with other students
- Violations result in F for exam and possible F for course

### Make-Up Policy
- Excused make-ups: Arranged in advance or serious documented excuse
- Unexcused make-ups: Penalty of at least one letter grade
- Late pass available (if not used)
- Must complete before next exam/assignment

## Tips for Success

### Before Exam
- Start studying 3-4 days in advance
- Review all readings
- Organize notes by topic
- Attend office hours with questions

### During Exam
- Read instructions carefully
- Budget time
- Start with familiar questions
- Outline essays before writing
- Check work if time permits
