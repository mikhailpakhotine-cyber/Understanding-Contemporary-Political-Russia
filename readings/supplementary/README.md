# Supplementary Materials

## Overview
Supplementary materials provide additional depth and breadth beyond required readings. These are optional but recommended for students seeking deeper understanding.

## Purpose

- Provide alternative perspectives
- Support research for papers
- Deepen engagement with material
- Prepare for advanced study

## Quality Guidelines

### Good Supplementary Sources
- Academically rigorous
- Well-documented
- Reputable publishers/authors
- Current and relevant
- Balanced perspective

### Sources to Avoid
- Wikipedia
- Unverified blogs
- Propaganda sites
- Outdated materials
- Questionable sources

## Citation Requirements

All supplementary materials used in assignments must be:
- Properly cited
- From reputable sources
- Documented consistently
- Verifiable
