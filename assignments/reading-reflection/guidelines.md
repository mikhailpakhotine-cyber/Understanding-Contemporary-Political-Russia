# Reading Reflection Assignment Guidelines

## Assignment Overview
Write a 1-2 page essay in response to either discussion questions or the web exercise at the end of an assigned chapter in the Shiraev textbook.

## Value
5 points

## Due Date
Varies by student - Check Canvas for your assigned due date
**Latest due date**: December 2, 2024, 11:59 PM

## Requirements

### Length
- Minimum: 1 full page
- Maximum: 2 pages
- **Important**: Papers that do not meet the minimum will receive a grade of D or lower
- Papers exceeding the maximum by more than 1 page will receive a grade of D or lower

### Format
- Typed
- Double-spaced
- 10- or 12-point font
- Standard margins (1 inch)

### Content
- Choose ONE chapter from Shiraev
- Respond to EITHER:
  - Discussion questions at the end of the chapter, OR
  - Web exercise at the end of the chapter
- Must focus on course content
- Must reference course readings

### Citations
- Properly document all sources
- Use an accepted citation style (APA or MLA)
- Consistent and correct citation throughout
- **No Wikipedia** or other encyclopedias

## Grading Criteria
See [rubric.md](rubric.md) for detailed scoring guide

### Citation Grading
- No documentation: Grade of F
- Incomplete/inconsistent documentation: Grade of D
- Incorrect citation style: Grade of C
- Using Wikipedia or questionable sources: Penalty of at least one letter grade

## Submission

### Where to Submit
- Canvas only (not via email)
- Keep a backup copy on your computer

### Turnitin
- All submissions are checked through Turnitin
- You may submit drafts to check for plagiarism
- No penalty for plagiarism in drafts
- Plagiarism in final submission will result in F for assignment

## Late Policy

### Standard Late Penalty
- 2 points deducted per calendar day late
- Must be submitted before next exam/assignment or last class session

### Late Pass
- You have ONE late pass for the semester
- Email professor by due date to invoke
- Provides one-week penalty-free extension

### Excused Absences
- Serious, documented excuses only
- Documentation must be provided
- Does not require use of late pass

## Getting Help

### Draft Review
- Professor will review hard copy drafts
- Must submit at least 2 weeks before due date
- Not available via email
- Office hours review limited to 10 minutes

### Resources
- Writing Center (free tutoring)
- Canvas "Helpful Writing Resources"
- Library reference librarians
- Anti-plagiarism guide on Canvas

## Academic Integrity
- All work must be your own
- Study and discuss with others, but write independently
- Plagiarism will result in F for assignment
- May result in F for course
- See syllabus for full policy

## Tips for Success
1. Read the chapter thoroughly before responding
2. Choose discussion questions or web exercise that interest you
3. Use specific examples from the reading
4. Support your arguments with evidence
5. Proofread carefully
6. Cite all sources properly
7. Submit on time or use your late pass strategically

## Questions?
- Review the syllabus
- Check the rubric
- Attend office hours
- Email professor with specific questions
