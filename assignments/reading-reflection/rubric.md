# Reading Reflection Rubric

**Total Points**: 5

## Scoring Categories

### Content & Analysis (3 points)

#### Excellent (3 points)
- Thoroughly addresses discussion questions or web exercise
- Demonstrates deep understanding of chapter content
- Provides specific examples from the reading
- Makes insightful connections and analysis
- Shows critical thinking

#### Good (2.5 points)
- Adequately addresses discussion questions or web exercise
- Shows understanding of chapter content
- Includes some specific examples
- Makes relevant connections
- Shows some critical thinking

#### Satisfactory (2 points)
- Partially addresses discussion questions or web exercise
- Shows basic understanding of chapter content
- Limited specific examples
- Minimal analysis or connections
- Limited critical thinking

#### Poor (1-1.5 points)
- Minimally addresses assignment
- Shows limited understanding
- Few or no specific examples
- Lacks analysis
- No critical thinking evident

#### Unacceptable (0-0.5 points)
- Does not address assignment
- Shows no understanding of content
- No examples from reading
- No analysis

### Writing Quality (1 point)

#### Excellent (1 point)
- Clear, organized structure
- Well-developed paragraphs
- Strong topic sentences
- Smooth transitions
- Professional academic tone
- Few or no grammatical/spelling errors

#### Good (0.75 points)
- Generally clear organization
- Developed paragraphs
- Adequate transitions
- Appropriate tone
- Minor grammatical/spelling errors

#### Satisfactory (0.5 points)
- Basic organization
- Somewhat developed paragraphs
- Some organizational issues
- Inconsistent tone
- Several grammatical/spelling errors

#### Poor (0.25 points)
- Unclear organization
- Poorly developed paragraphs
- Weak transitions
- Inappropriate tone
- Many grammatical/spelling errors

#### Unacceptable (0 points)
- No clear organization
- No paragraph development
- Difficult to follow
- Many errors that interfere with understanding

### Citations & Format (1 point)

#### Excellent (1 point)
- Correct, consistent use of accepted citation style (APA or MLA)
- All sources properly documented
- Meets all format requirements (length, spacing, font, margins)
- Uses reputable sources only

#### Good (0.75 points)
- Generally correct citation style
- Most sources documented
- Meets format requirements
- Uses appropriate sources

#### Satisfactory (0.5 points)
- Inconsistent citation style (Grade penalty: C)
- Some sources not documented (Grade penalty: D)
- Minor format issues
- Mostly appropriate sources

#### Poor (0.25 points)
- Incorrect citation style (Grade penalty: C)
- Many sources not documented (Grade penalty: D)
- Multiple format violations
- Some questionable sources

#### Unacceptable (0 points)
- No citations (Grade penalty: F)
- No sources documented (Grade penalty: F)
- Does not meet basic format requirements
- Uses Wikipedia or obviously questionable sources (one letter grade penalty)

## Additional Grading Notes

### Length Requirements
- **Below minimum** (less than 1 full page): Grade of D or lower
- **Exceeds maximum** (more than 3 pages): Grade of D or lower
- Papers must meet length requirements to receive above D

### Plagiarism
- Any plagiarism detected: F for assignment
- May result in F for course
- No "second chances" after final submission

### Focus on Course Content
- Must reference course readings
- Outside sources allowed but should not replace course materials
- Must connect to course themes

## Common Issues to Avoid

1. **Plagiarism** - Always cite sources and write in your own words
2. **Too short/long** - Carefully check page count before submitting
3. **No citations** - Even paraphrasing requires citation
4. **Wikipedia** - Never use as a source
5. **Generic response** - Must engage specifically with the chapter
6. **Poor proofreading** - Always review before submitting
7. **Late submission** - Submit on time or use late pass
