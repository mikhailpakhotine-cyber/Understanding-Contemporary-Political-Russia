# Travel Itinerary Research Paper Guidelines

## Assignment Overview
"Plan a trip to Russia" and write up a descriptive travel itinerary or journal. The itinerary must be centered on a theme and demonstrate research into Russian culture, history, and society.

## Value
20 points (20% of total grade)

## Due Date
**November 18, 2024, at 11:59 PM** on Canvas

## Requirements

### Length
- Minimum: 5 full pages
- Maximum: 7 pages
- **Important**: Papers that do not meet the minimum will receive a grade of D or lower
- Papers exceeding the maximum by more than 1 page will receive a grade of D or lower

### Format
- Typed
- Double-spaced
- 10- or 12-point font
- Standard margins (1 inch)

### Theme Requirement
Your itinerary MUST be centered on a **specific theme**. Examples include:
- Art and architecture
- Music and performing arts
- World War II history
- Science and technology
- Russian literature
- Military history
- Religious heritage
- Soviet history
- Imperial Russia
- Regional cultures
- Film and cinema
- **Other themes** - confirm with professor if unsure

### Content Requirements

The itinerary should include:

1. **Introduction**
   - Your chosen theme
   - Why you selected this theme
   - Overview of planned trip

2. **Detailed Itinerary**
   - Specific locations/sites to visit
   - Why each location is significant to your theme
   - What you would do/see at each location
   - Historical or cultural context for each site
   - Practical details (cities, regions, approximate timing)

3. **Cultural/Historical Analysis**
   - How sites relate to Russian culture and society
   - Historical significance
   - Contemporary relevance
   - Connection to course themes

4. **Conclusion**
   - What you would learn from this trip
   - How it deepens understanding of Russia

### Research Requirements
- Must use **at least 5 reputable sources**
- Course textbook may be one source
- Academic sources preferred (books, scholarly articles, museum websites, government sites)
- Travel guides can supplement but shouldn't be primary sources
- **No Wikipedia** - will result in at least one letter grade penalty

### Citations
- Properly document all sources
- Use an accepted citation style (APA or MLA)
- Consistent and correct citation throughout
- Include Works Cited/References page (doesn't count toward page limit)

## Tips for Success

### Choosing a Theme
1. Pick something you're genuinely interested in
2. Make sure there are enough sites/locations for 5-7 pages
3. Confirm sufficient research sources are available
4. Consider breadth - too narrow makes it hard to fill pages, too broad makes it hard to focus

### Research Strategy
1. Start early - don't wait until November!
2. Use library databases for scholarly sources
3. Museum and cultural institution websites are excellent resources
4. Government tourism sites can provide practical information
5. Cross-reference information across multiple sources

### Writing the Itinerary
1. Balance description with analysis
2. Include specific details (names, locations, dates)
3. Explain WHY each site matters to your theme
4. Connect to what you've learned in class
5. Make it engaging - you're "planning a trip"
6. Use transitions to connect different stops/days

### Before Submitting
1. Check page count carefully
2. Proofread thoroughly
3. Verify all citations are formatted correctly
4. Ensure References/Works Cited page is complete
5. Confirm you have at least 5 sources
6. Check that theme is clear throughout
7. Submit with time to spare (don't wait until 11:59!)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too generic** - Needs a specific theme, not just "famous places in Russia"
2. **Insufficient research** - Must use at least 5 quality sources
3. **Poor organization** - Should flow logically
4. **No analysis** - Don't just list places, explain their significance
5. **Wikipedia use** - Will result in grade penalty
6. **Last-minute rush** - Quality suffers when rushed
7. **Wrong length** - Carefully check page requirements
8. **Missing citations** - Everything needs proper documentation
