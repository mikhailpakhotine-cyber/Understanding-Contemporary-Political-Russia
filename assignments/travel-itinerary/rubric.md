# Travel Itinerary Research Paper Rubric

**Total Points**: 20

## Scoring Categories

### 1. Research Quality and Depth (6 points)

#### Excellent (5.5-6 points)
- Uses 5+ high-quality, reputable sources
- Demonstrates extensive research
- Sources are scholarly and appropriate
- Information is accurate and well-integrated
- Shows deep engagement with research materials

#### Good (5-5.4 points)
- Uses 5+ reputable sources
- Demonstrates solid research
- Appropriate sources
- Information generally accurate
- Good integration of research

#### Satisfactory (4-4.9 points)
- Uses minimum 5 sources
- Adequate research
- Some sources may be less scholarly
- Basic accuracy
- Research somewhat integrated

#### Poor (3-3.9 points)
- Fewer than 5 sources OR questionable source quality
- Limited research
- Some inaccuracies
- Poor integration of sources

#### Unacceptable (0-2.9 points)
- Insufficient sources
- Poor quality sources
- Significant inaccuracies
- Little evidence of research

### 2. Theme Development and Focus (5 points)

#### Excellent (4.5-5 points)
- Clear, specific theme consistently maintained throughout
- All sites/locations directly relate to theme
- Strong thematic coherence
- Insightful analysis of theme in Russian context
- Demonstrates why theme matters

#### Good (4-4.4 points)
- Clear theme maintained throughout
- Sites relate to theme
- Good thematic coherence
- Adequate analysis of theme
- Explains theme relevance

#### Satisfactory (3.5-3.9 points)
- Theme present but may waver
- Most sites relate to theme
- Some thematic coherence
- Basic analysis
- Limited explanation of relevance

#### Poor (3-3.4 points)
- Weak or unclear theme
- Some sites don't relate to theme
- Lacks coherence
- Minimal analysis
- Theme relevance unclear

#### Unacceptable (0-2.9 points)
- No clear theme
- Sites don't relate to stated theme
- No coherence
- No thematic analysis

### 3. Writing Quality and Organization (4 points)

#### Excellent (3.5-4 points)
- Clear, logical organization
- Engaging, descriptive writing
- Strong introduction and conclusion
- Smooth transitions between sections
- Well-developed paragraphs
- Professional academic tone
- Very few grammatical/spelling errors

#### Good (3-3.4 points)
- Good organization
- Clear writing
- Adequate introduction and conclusion
- Generally smooth transitions
- Developed paragraphs
- Appropriate tone
- Minor errors

#### Satisfactory (2.5-2.9 points)
- Basic organization
- Adequate writing
- Introduction and conclusion present
- Some organizational issues
- Paragraphs developed
- Generally appropriate tone
- Several errors

#### Poor (2-2.4 points)
- Weak organization
- Unclear writing
- Weak introduction/conclusion
- Poor transitions
- Underdeveloped paragraphs
- Inconsistent tone
- Many errors

#### Unacceptable (0-1.9 points)
- No clear organization
- Difficult to follow
- Missing introduction or conclusion
- No transitions
- Poor paragraph development
- Many errors interfering with meaning

### 4. Citations and Format (3 points)

#### Excellent (2.7-3 points)
- Perfect or near-perfect citation style (APA or MLA)
- All sources properly documented in text and references
- Meets all format requirements (5-7 pages, spacing, font, margins)
- Complete References/Works Cited page
- No Wikipedia or questionable sources

#### Good (2.4-2.6 points)
- Correct citation style with minor errors
- Most sources properly documented
- Meets format requirements
- Complete references page
- Appropriate sources

#### Satisfactory (2-2.3 points)
- Citation style inconsistent (Note: may result in C grade)
- Some sources not fully documented (Note: may result in D grade)
- Minor format issues
- References page mostly complete
- Mostly appropriate sources

#### Poor (1.5-1.9 points)
- Incorrect citation style (Note: results in C grade max)
- Many sources not documented (Note: may result in D grade)
- Multiple format violations
- Incomplete references
- Some questionable sources

#### Unacceptable (0-1.4 points)
- No citations (Note: results in F)
- No documentation (Note: results in F)
- Major format violations
- Missing references page
- Wikipedia or obviously questionable sources (Note: one letter grade penalty)

### 5. Descriptive Detail and Creativity (2 points)

#### Excellent (1.8-2 points)
- Vivid, specific descriptions of sites
- Creative and engaging presentation
- Strong sense of place
- Demonstrates enthusiasm for topic
- Makes reader want to take the trip

#### Good (1.6-1.7 points)
- Good descriptive details
- Engaging presentation
- Sense of place
- Interest in topic evident
- Generally appealing

#### Satisfactory (1.4-1.5 points)
- Adequate descriptions
- Basic presentation
- Some sense of place
- Shows some interest

#### Poor (1-1.3 points)
- Limited descriptions
- Generic presentation
- Little sense of place
- Minimal engagement

#### Unacceptable (0-0.9 points)
- No descriptive detail
- No creativity
- List of places without description
- No engagement

## Additional Requirements and Penalties

### Length Requirements
- **Below minimum** (less than 5 full pages): Grade of D or lower
- **Exceeds maximum** (more than 8 pages): Grade of D or lower

### Plagiarism
- Any plagiarism detected: F for assignment
- May result in F for course

### Source Requirements
- Fewer than 5 sources: Deduction in Research Quality score
- Wikipedia use: Minimum one letter grade penalty
- Questionable sources: Grade reduction

### Connection to Course
- Must demonstrate understanding of Russian culture/society/history
- Should connect to course themes where appropriate
- Pure travel guide approach without cultural analysis will score lower

## Excellence Indicators

Papers earning A grades typically:
- Demonstrate extensive, high-quality research
- Maintain sharp focus on a specific, interesting theme
- Show deep cultural/historical understanding
- Are extremely well-written and organized
- Include vivid, engaging descriptions
- Make strong connections to Russian society
- Have perfect or near-perfect citations
- Show creativity and genuine interest

## Common Issues That Lower Grades

1. **Generic theme** - "Famous places" instead of specific focus
2. **Insufficient research** - Relying on 1-2 sources
3. **List format** - Just listing places without analysis
4. **Poor citations** - Missing or incorrect documentation
5. **Too short/long** - Not meeting page requirements
6. **Surface-level** - No depth of cultural understanding
7. **Travel guide approach** - Practical info without historical/cultural context
8. **Last-minute work** - Shows in quality
