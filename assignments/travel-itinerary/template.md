## Cultural Analysis

[2-3 paragraphs analyzing broader themes]

**Paragraph 1**: Common themes across sites
- What patterns do you notice?
- What do these sites collectively reveal?
- How do they connect to each other?

**Paragraph 2**: Contemporary relevance
- How do these sites matter to Russia today?
- What do they show about Russian political culture?
- Connection to course themes (political culture, national identity, etc.)

**Paragraph 3**: Comparative perspective
- How does this aspect of Russian culture compare to the United States or other countries?
- What is distinctively Russian about these sites/this theme?

*Example*:
"Across all these World War II memorials, a common theme emerges: the centrality of sacrifice and collective memory to Russian national identity. Unlike American WWII memorials, which often focus on individual heroism, Russian memorials emphasize collective struggle and victory (Citation). This reflects the broader Russian political culture that Shiraev (2013) describes as emphasizing collective good over individualism..."

## Additional Tips

### Making It Descriptive and Engaging
- Use specific details (names, dates, architectural features)
- Include sensory details where appropriate
- Show enthusiasm for your theme
- Make the reader want to take this trip

### Balancing Description and Analysis
- Don't just describe - explain WHY things matter
- Connect each site to broader Russian culture
- Link to course concepts
- Show critical thinking

### Strong Themes That Work Well
- Specific enough to maintain focus
- Broad enough for 5-7 pages
- Rich enough for research
- Interesting to you personally

### Research Sources That Work Well
- Museum websites
- Academic books on Russian culture/history
- Scholarly articles
- Government cultural sites
- UNESCO World Heritage descriptions
- Academic travel guides (as supplements)
