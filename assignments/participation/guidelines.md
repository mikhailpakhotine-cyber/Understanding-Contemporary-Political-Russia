# Class Participation Guidelines

## Assignment Overview
Active, positive participation in all class discussions and activities throughout the semester.

## Value
15 points (15% of total grade)

## Duration
Ongoing throughout the semester

## What Counts as Participation?

### Attendance
- Regular, prompt attendance at all class sessions
- Arriving on time
- Staying for the entire class session
- Being present on non-exam days

**Important**: If you are not there, you cannot participate!

### Active Engagement
Participation means MORE than just attendance. It includes:
- Contributing to class discussions
- Asking thoughtful questions
- Responding to others' comments
- Participating in small group activities
- Engaging in pair work
- Sharing in whole-class discussions
- Completing in-class activities
- Taking notes and staying focused

### Quality Participation Examples
- Building on others' ideas
- Asking clarifying questions
- Providing evidence-based responses
- Making connections between concepts
- Offering thoughtful analysis
- Respectful disagreement with reasoning
- Sharing relevant insights

## What Does NOT Count as Good Participation?

### Negative Participation
Behaviors that detract from class:
- Inappropriate talking during class
- Refusing to follow professor's instructions
- Disrespect for others' opinions
- Monopolizing discussions
- Using offensive language
- Making inappropriate remarks (racist, sexist, etc.)
- Disturbing class when entering or leaving
- Cell phone disruptions

### Passive Attendance
- Simply being present without contributing
- Not engaging in discussions
- Not participating in group work
- Remaining silent throughout semester
- Texting or using phone during class
- Inappropriate use of laptop/technology
- Sleeping or appearing disengaged

## Grading Rubric
See [rubric.md](rubric.md) for detailed scoring guide

### Components
- **Attendance** (7 points): Based on number of absences
- **Quality of Participation** (8 points): Based on engagement level

## Attendance Policy

### Excused vs. Unexcused Absences
**Important**: For participation grading, there is NO distinction between excused and unexcused absences. If you are not there, you cannot participate, regardless of reason.

**Exception**: Extended absences for medical reasons - contact professor immediately

### Exam Day Attendance
- Attendance on exam days does NOT impact participation score
- Must be present to take exam (separate issue)

### Tardiness and Early Departure
- Frequent tardiness lowers participation grade
- Frequently leaving before class ends lowers participation grade
- **Your responsibility** to ensure you're marked present if arriving late

## University-Approved Extracurricular Activities

### What Qualifies
- On-campus activities (athletics, Model UN, student government, etc.)
- Must provide supporting documentation IN ADVANCE
- "Reasonable number" of absences (professor determines)

### Requirements for Student-Athletes
- Excused for **competitions only**
- **NOT excused for**: practices, team meetings, off-season events
- All assignments must be made up IN ADVANCE

### Off-Campus Activities
- Generally not excused (difficult to verify)
- Considered on case-by-case basis

**Remember**: Extracurricular activities should never interfere with curricular activities!

## Technology in the Classroom

### Permitted Uses
- Note-taking on laptops/tablets
- Accessing course readings
- Participating in class activities
- Educational purposes only

### Prohibited Uses
- Checking email
- Playing games
- Texting
- Social media
- Non-class websites
- Any use that distracts others

### Consequences of Misuse
- Loss of permission to bring technology to class
- Lowered participation grade
- Possible removal from course (per Student Conduct process)

### Recording
- **Requires written permission** from professor in advance
- Applies to all recording technology (Livescribe pens, audio recorders, etc.)
- Taking pictures or video-recording is NOT permitted

## Creating a Respectful Classroom

### Professor's Commitment
- Treat all students with respect
- Welcome differing opinions
- Create safe space for discussion
- Analytical, not personal, approach

### Student Responsibilities
- Treat classmates with respect
- Treat professor with respect
- Welcome others' viewpoints
- Engage in reasoned, analytical discussion
- Support opinions with evidence
- Listen actively to others

### Discussion Norms
- Political science topics can be controversial
- Discussions are analytical, not emotional
- All opinions must be supported with reasoned arguments
- Respect required even (especially!) when disagreeing
- No personal attacks

## Disruptive Behavior

### Definition
"Substantial disruption of University operations including obstruction of teaching, research, administration, other University activities..."

### Examples
- Inappropriate talking
- Refusal to follow instructions
- Disrespecting others' opinions
- Monopolizing discussions
- Offensive language or remarks
- Disturbing class entry/exit
- Cell phone issues
- Inappropriate technology use
- Texting during class
- Distracting classmates

### Consequences
- Warning
- Lowered participation grade
- Failing participation grade
- Continued behavior: failing course grade
- Removal from course (per Student Conduct process)

### Serious Violations
Bullying, verbal abuse, threats (in person, email, Canvas, social media), or physical threats/harm:
- Result in removal from course
- Processed through Student Conduct

## Tips for Strong Participation

### Preparation
- Complete all readings before class
- Take notes on readings
- Prepare questions in advance
- Review previous class notes

### During Class
- Arrive on time
- Sit where you can see/hear/engage
- Take notes actively
- Put away phone
- Make eye contact
- Lean forward/show engagement
- Raise hand to contribute
- Listen actively to others

### Contributing to Discussion
- Speak up even if nervous
- Build on others' ideas
- Ask questions when confused
- Share relevant insights
- Reference readings when possible
- Be respectful but don't be afraid to disagree
- Help create welcoming environment for all

### Group Work
- Contribute your share
- Listen to group members
- Stay on task
- Help group succeed
- Share findings with class when asked

## Tracking Your Participation

### Self-Assessment Questions
Ask yourself regularly:
- Am I attending consistently?
- Am I arriving on time?
- Do I contribute to discussions?
- Do I participate in group work?
- Am I engaged and focused?
- Do I ask questions?
- Do I help create a positive classroom environment?

### If You're Struggling
- Review participation rubric
- Assess your engagement honestly
- Make specific goals
- Talk to professor in office hours
- Seek help if anxiety/other issues interfere

## Special Circumstances

### Disability Accommodations
- Register with Disability Resource Center (DRC)
- Initiate accommodation release through Clockwork
- Notify professor of accommodations
- Remind professor before each use

### Anxiety/Speaking Issues
- Talk to professor about concerns
- Alternative ways to show engagement
- Written contributions may be possible
- DRC may be able to help

### Extended Absences
- Medical situations
- Family emergencies
- Contact professor immediately
- Provide documentation
- Work out plan to stay current

## Questions?
- Review the rubric
- Assess your current participation
- Attend office hours
- Email with specific concerns
