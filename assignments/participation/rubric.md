# Class Participation Rubric

**Total Points**: 15

## Scoring Components

### 1. Attendance (7 points)

Attendance is scored based on the number of absences throughout the semester.

| Absences | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 0-1 absences | 7 points | Excellent attendance |
| 2 absences | 6 points | Very good attendance |
| 3 absences | 5 points | Good attendance |
| 4 absences | 4 points | Acceptable attendance |
| 5 absences | 3 points | Poor attendance |
| 6 absences | 2 points | Very poor attendance |
| 7 absences | 1 point | Unacceptable attendance |
| 8+ absences | 0 points | Failing attendance |

#### Important Notes on Attendance Points
- **No distinction** between excused and unexcused absences (exception: extended medical absences)
- Exam days do NOT count toward participation score
- Frequent tardiness may lower score
- Leaving early frequently may lower score
- Student must ensure they are marked present if arriving late

### 2. Quality of Participation (8 points)

#### Excellent (7-8 points)
**Consistently active and engaged contributor**
- Attends regularly AND participates actively
- Contributes frequently to class discussions
- Asks thoughtful, relevant questions
- Responds constructively to others' ideas
- Actively participates in all group activities
- Demonstrates preparation through comments
- Helps create positive learning environment
- Shows critical thinking in contributions
- Respectful of all viewpoints
- References course readings appropriately

#### Good (5.5-6.5 points)
**Regular, solid contributor**
- Attends regularly and usually participates
- Contributes regularly to discussions
- Asks questions periodically
- Participates in group activities
- Shows preparation
- Respectful and engaged
- Makes relevant contributions
- Occasional critical analysis

#### Satisfactory (4-5 points)
**Adequate but inconsistent participant**
- Attendance may be inconsistent
- Participates occasionally
- Sometimes contributes to discussions
- Sporadic questions
- Participates when directly called upon
- Some evidence of preparation
- Generally respectful
- Contributions often basic

#### Poor (2-3.5 points)
**Minimal participation**
- Spotty attendance
- Rarely participates voluntarily
- Minimal contribution to discussions
- Rarely asks questions
- Reluctant participation in groups
- Limited evidence of preparation
- Passive presence
- May have some disruptive behaviors

#### Unacceptable (0-1.5 points)
**Little to no participation OR negative participation**
- Very poor attendance
- No voluntary participation
- Silent throughout semester
- Does not engage in activities
- No evidence of preparation
- Disengaged from class
- **OR** Disruptive behaviors that detract from class
- Negative impact on classroom environment

## Negative Participation Deductions

Disruptive or inappropriate behaviors result in point deductions:

| Behavior | Typical Deduction |
|----------|-------------------|
| Cell phone ringing/use during class | -0.5 to -1 point per incident |
| Inappropriate talking during class | -0.5 to -1 point per incident |
| Texting during class | -0.5 to -1 point per incident |
| Inappropriate technology use | -0.5 to -2 points |
| Disrespecting others' opinions | -1 to -3 points |
| Monopolizing discussions (after warnings) | -1 to -2 points |
| Arriving late/leaving early (pattern) | -0.5 to -1 point per occurrence |
| Refusal to follow instructions | -1 to -3 points |
| Offensive language/remarks | -2 to -5 points |
| Continued disruptions after warnings | -3 to -8 points |
| Serious violations (bullying, threats, abuse) | Failing grade + removal |

## Examples of Strong Participation

### Example 1: Student earning 14-15 points
- Misses 1 class (6.5-7 pts attendance)
- Contributes 2-3 substantive comments per class
- Asks clarifying questions regularly
- Actively engages in all group work
- Builds on others' ideas
- References readings in discussions
- Respectful of all viewpoints
- Arrives prepared and on time
- Total: 7 (attendance) + 7.5 (quality) = 14.5 points

### Example 2: Student earning 11-12 points
- Misses 3 classes (5 pts attendance)
- Contributes 1-2 comments most classes
- Occasionally asks questions
- Participates in group work
- Generally prepared
- Respectful
- Total: 5 (attendance) + 6 (quality) = 11 points

### Example 3: Student earning 7-9 points
- Misses 5 classes (3 pts attendance)
- Rarely speaks voluntarily
- Minimal group participation
- Arrives late several times
- Sometimes appears unprepared
- Total: 3 (attendance) + 4.5 (quality) = 7.5 points

### Example 4: Student with excellent attendance but poor participation
- Misses 0 classes (7 pts attendance)
- Never volunteers comments
- Silent in group work
- Never asks questions
- Passive presence only
- Total: 7 (attendance) + 2 (quality) = 9 points

## Self-Assessment Guide

### How Am I Doing?

Ask yourself these questions monthly:

**Attendance (7 points possible)**
- How many classes have I missed?
- Am I arriving on time?
- Am I staying for full class?

**Quality (8 points possible)**
- How often do I contribute to discussions?
- Do I ask questions when unclear?
- Do I participate actively in group work?
- Am I helping create a positive environment?
- Do I come prepared?
- Am I engaging with the material?

**Red Flags**
- Missing more than 3 classes
- Never speaking in class
- Using phone/technology inappropriately
- Not participating in group activities
- Arriving late frequently
- Appearing disengaged

## Improvement Strategies

### If Your Attendance Score Is Low
- Prioritize attending class
- Arrange schedule to avoid conflicts
- Use university resources if health/personal issues interfere
- Contact professor about extended absences

### If Your Participation Quality Is Low
- Set a goal to speak once per class
- Prepare one question from readings
- Volunteer for group presentations
- Sit closer to front
- Put away phone/laptop
- Come to office hours to discuss concerns

## Special Considerations

### Accommodations
Students with approved DRC accommodations should:
- Notify professor at semester start
- Discuss any participation-related accommodations
- Remind professor as needed

### Anxiety/Shyness
If speaking in class is difficult:
- Start small (one comment per class)
- Prepare comments in advance
- Participate in small groups first
- Talk to professor about concerns
- Consider whether DRC could help

### Language Barriers
If English is not your first language:
- Participation still expected
- Quality matters more than perfection
- Professor understands language challenges
- Writing comments may supplement

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Thinking attendance alone is enough** - Must also engage actively
2. **Monopolizing discussions** - Share space with others
3. **Using technology inappropriately** - Detracts from score
4. **Arriving late regularly** - Shows lack of commitment
5. **Never speaking** - Can't earn high quality score
6. **Being disrespectful** - Results in deductions
7. **Waiting until end of semester** - Participation is ongoing

## Questions About Your Participation Grade?
- Review this rubric carefully
- Do honest self-assessment
- Track your own attendance
- Attend office hours to discuss
