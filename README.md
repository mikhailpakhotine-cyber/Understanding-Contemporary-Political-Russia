## Course Overview

This course explores politics and society in contemporary Russia, covering:
- **Political Culture**: Russian governance systems, political attitudes and beliefs
- **Economic Transition**: Post-Soviet economic reforms and policies
- **Social Change**: Democratization, capitalism, and societal transformation

As a Foreign Culture course, we compare Russian systems and attitudes with those of the United States, examining how post-Soviet Russia's reforms align or differ from US systems.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
1. **Comparative and International Politics**: Demonstrate knowledge of concepts in comparative and international politics
2. **Critical Thinking**: Effectively apply critical thinking and problem-solving to political issues
3. **Writing**: Communicate clearly and effectively about political topics in a scholarly manner
