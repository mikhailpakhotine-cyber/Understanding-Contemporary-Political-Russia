# Citation Style Guides

## Overview
This course accepts both APA and MLA citation styles. You must use ONE style consistently throughout any given paper.

## Choosing a Style

### APA (American Psychological Association)
- Common in social sciences
- Emphasizes publication date
- Uses author-date citations
- **Recommended for this course**

### MLA (Modern Language Association)
- Common in humanities
- Emphasizes author and page number
- Uses author-page citations
- Also acceptable for this course

**Important**: Pick one and use it consistently!

## Quick Reference Guides

### APA Style Basics

#### In-Text Citations

**Single author**:
(Shiraev, 2013)
OR
Shiraev (2013) argues that...

**Direct quote**:
(Shiraev, 2013, p. 45)

**Multiple authors**:
(Smith & Jones, 2015)
(Anderson et al., 2014) [for 3+ authors]

#### Reference List Format

**Book**:
Shiraev, E. (2013). *Russian government and politics* (2nd ed.). Palgrave Macmillan/St. Martin's Press.

**Journal article**:
Smith, J. (2015). Political culture in Russia. *Journal of Politics*, 45(2), 123-145. https://doi.org/xxxxx

**Website**:
Author, A. (Year). Title of page. *Website Name*. URL

### MLA Style Basics

#### In-Text Citations

**Single author**:
(Shiraev 45)
OR
Shiraev argues that... (45).

**Multiple authors**:
(Smith and Jones 23)
(Anderson et al. 67) [for 3+ authors]

#### Works Cited Format

**Book**:
Shiraev, Eric. *Russian Government and Politics*. 2nd ed., Palgrave Macmillan/St. Martin's Press, 2013.

**Journal article**:
Smith, John. "Political Culture in Russia." *Journal of Politics*, vol. 45, no. 2, 2015, pp. 123-145.

**Website**:
Author. "Title of Page." *Website Name*, Date, URL.

## Common Citation Scenarios

### Textbook (Shiraev)

**APA**:
- In-text: (Shiraev, 2013) or (Shiraev, 2013, p. 45)
- Reference: Shiraev, E. (2013). *Russian government and politics* (2nd ed.). Palgrave Macmillan/St. Martin's Press.

**MLA**:
- In-text: (Shiraev 45)
- Works Cited: Shiraev, Eric. *Russian Government and Politics*. 2nd ed., Palgrave Macmillan/St. Martin's Press, 2013.

### Museum Website

**APA**:
- In-text: (Hermitage Museum, 2020)
- Reference: Hermitage Museum. (2020). *Collection overview*. https://www.hermitagemuseum.org

**MLA**:
- In-text: ("Collection Overview")
- Works Cited: "Collection Overview." *Hermitage Museum*, 2020, www.hermitagemuseum.org.

### Online Article (with author)

**APA**:
- In-text: (Jones, 2022)
- Reference: Jones, M. (2022, January 15). Understanding Russian politics. *Policy Review*. https://www.example.com

**MLA**:
- In-text: (Jones)
- Works Cited: Jones, Mary. "Understanding Russian Politics." *Policy Review*, 15 Jan. 2022, www.example.com.

### Online Article (no author)

**APA**:
- In-text: ("Understanding Russian Politics," 2022)
- Reference: Understanding Russian politics. (2022, January 15). *Policy Review*. https://www.example.com

**MLA**:
- In-text: ("Understanding Russian Politics")
- Works Cited: "Understanding Russian Politics." *Policy Review*, 15 Jan. 2022, www.example.com.

## Formatting Guidelines

### APA Format

**Title Page**:
- Title of paper
- Your name
- Course and section
- Date
- Page number in header

**Reference List**:
- New page at end
- Title: "References" (centered)
- Alphabetical by author last name
- Hanging indent
- Double-spaced

### MLA Format

**First Page**:
- Your name
- Professor's name
- Course
- Date
- Title (centered)

**Works Cited**:
- New page at end
- Title: "Works Cited" (centered)
- Alphabetical by author last name
- Hanging indent
- Double-spaced

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Mixing styles** - Pick APA or MLA, not both
2. **Inconsistent formatting** - Follow format throughout
3. **Missing citations** - Cite everything that needs it
4. **No page numbers for quotes** - Always include
5. **Incorrect hanging indent** - Use proper formatting
6. **Alphabetizing by first name** - Use last name
7. **Wikipedia citations** - Never allowed in this course

## Special Cases

### Multiple Works by Same Author
**APA**: Order by year
**MLA**: Order by title, use three hyphens for repeated author

### No Date Available
**APA**: (Author, n.d.)
**MLA**: Omit date

### No Author
**APA**: Start with title
**MLA**: Start with title

### Personal Communication/Interview
**APA**: Cite in text only, not in references
**MLA**: Include in Works Cited
