## How to Use Rubrics

### Before Starting Assignment
1. Read rubric carefully
2. Understand point distribution
3. Note key criteria
4. Plan to address all components

### While Working
1. Refer back to rubric
2. Self-assess as you go
3. Ensure all criteria met
4. Focus on high-point categories

### Before Submitting
1. Final rubric check
2. Verify all requirements met
3. Self-score honestly
4. Revise weak areas

## Grading Criteria Summary

### Reading Reflection (5 points)
- Content & Analysis: 3 points
- Writing Quality: 1 point
- Citations & Format: 1 point

### Travel Itinerary (20 points)
- Research Quality: 6 points
- Theme Development: 5 points
- Writing Quality: 4 points
- Citations & Format: 3 points
- Descriptive Detail: 2 points

### Participation (15 points)
- Attendance: 7 points
- Quality of Participation: 8 points

## General Grading Standards

### Excellent Work (A Range)
- Exceeds expectations
- Deep understanding
- Excellent writing
- Perfect or near-perfect citations
- Shows critical thinking

### Good Work (B Range)
- Meets all expectations
- Clear understanding
- Good writing
- Correct citations
- Shows solid thinking

### Satisfactory Work (C Range)
- Meets basic expectations
- Adequate understanding
- Acceptable writing
- Generally correct citations
- Shows basic thinking

### Poor Work (D Range)
- Fails to meet some expectations
- Limited understanding
- Weak writing
- Citation problems
- Limited thinking

### Failing Work (F)
- Doesn't meet expectations
- Lacks understanding
- Poor writing
- Missing/incorrect citations
- No critical thinking
- OR academic integrity violation

## Citation Penalties

Across all assignments:
- No citations: F
- Incomplete citations: D
- Incorrect style: C
- Inconsistent style: C
- Wikipedia use: One letter grade penalty
